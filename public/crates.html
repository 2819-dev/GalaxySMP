How to get keys

Common keyFinish all 3 daily quests (/quests), or buy one at the Common Crate at spawn.
Rare keyDay 7 of your daily reward streak (/daily), the Season Pass, or inside a Common Crate.
Galaxy keyEvents, server goals, the Season Pass, or a lucky Rare Crate.

Open a crate by tapping it at spawn with a key in your inventory.
