Features

What's on GalaxySMP

Everything you can do on the server, all in one world.

Proximity voice chat, built in

Talk to players near you in game: voices get quieter as people walk away, and come from the direction they're standing. Create a group to talk from anywhere. No mods and no extra accounts: it uses your linked GalaxySMP account.

Open Voice
  • Talking nearby
  • Talking in a group
  • Global Mic (events)
  • Muted
  • Deafened
  • Not in voice

Actively moderated

Messages are screened for inappropriate language, links and personal information, and staff review every report.

Privacy first

Accounts need only a Minecraft username and a password. We never ask for email addresses, phone numbers or birthdays, and you choose who can see your name.

Verified accounts

Each account is confirmed in game with a one-time code, so no one can impersonate another player.
