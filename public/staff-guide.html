Running the Community

A short guide for GalaxySMP staff. Open your panel from the Community sidebar: owners see the Control Panel, admins the Admin Panel and other staff the Staff Panel. It's the same page; it just shows the tools you're allowed to use.

Overview

The first tab shows live numbers (server status, open tickets and reports, players, members, plans, punishments this week), who's in game right now, quick buttons, an in-game announcement box (it shows at the top of everyone's screen with a chime) and recent staff activity.

Support tickets

  • Players open a ticket in Community → Support. It's a private live chat between them and staff.
  • Open tickets show in the Tickets tab, with a number on the tab and next to the panel link. Urgent and high priority tickets are shown first.
  • Open one to reply. Your reply reaches the player instantly. Use the dropdowns to set the status (Needs staff, Waiting for player, Resolved, Closed), priority (Urgent, High, Normal, Low) and label (Question, Bug, Player report, Appeal, Store, Other). Take it marks you as the one handling it.
  • When the player answers, the ticket goes back to "Needs staff". Closed tickets can't get new player messages; they can open a new one.

Players

  • Search by gamertag or website name, or filter (in game now, staff, plans, punished). Tap a player for their card.
  • The card shows stats, money, rank, plan, website account, punishment history, reports against them, their support tickets and private staff notes (only staff can see notes; only admins can delete them).
  • In game: Warn, Mute, Unmute and Kick (all staff), Ban and Unban (admins). These need the server to be online.
  • Admins can also change the player's rank, plan and money and give crate keys right from the card.
  • You can't act on server owners, operators, or staff at your own level or higher (owners can).

Store items (Admins)

The Store has two sections: Subscriptions (Galaxy+ and Galaxy Pro+) and Shop (money, extra plots and shop plots, Night Vision, particle trails and join effects). Until payments open, give items from Players → a player → Give a Shop item. Limits are checked for you (for example, only 2 extra plots). Players choose trails and join effects in game with /cosmetics.

Staff powers are hidden

Moderator and Admin are only permissions, set on a rank ("Staff powers"). Players never see them: only the rank's name, color and icon show, on the website and in game.

Roles

  • Member: everyone starts here. Can chat, react, reply and report messages.
  • Moderator: can delete messages, mute members, handle reports, edit the word filter, read the audit log and post in #announcements.
  • Admin: everything a Moderator can do, plus banning and changing roles.

Staff can only act on people with a lower role than their own. Server owners (set in the website's settings) can never be muted, banned or demoted from the website.

Handling reports

  1. When someone reports a message, a red number appears next to Staff tools in the chat sidebar.
  2. Open Panel → Reports. Each card shows the message, who wrote it, and who reported it and why.
  3. Choose one:
    • Delete message if it breaks the rules. This also closes the report.
    • Mute author if they need a break from chatting.
    • Mark resolved if you dealt with it another way (for example, a word in game).
    • Dismiss if nothing was wrong.

Deleting a message

In chat, hover over the message (on a phone, tap it) and select the trash icon. Add a short reason. The message disappears for everyone straight away. Staff can still see what it said in the audit log.

Muting a member

  1. In chat, tap the member's name in the member list, or go to Panel → Members and search for them.
  2. Select Mute, choose a length (10 minutes, 1 hour, 1 day, 7 days or 30 days) and add a reason.
  3. They can still read chat but can't send messages until the mute ends. To end it early, select Unmute.

Suggested approach: a warning first, then 10 minutes, then 1 hour, then 1 day. Save bans for serious or repeated problems.

Banning a member (Admins)

  1. Tap the member's name in chat, or find them in Panel → Members.
  2. Select Ban and enter a reason (required).
  3. They are signed out immediately and can't use the community. To reverse it, filter Members by Banned and select Unban.

A website ban doesn't ban them from the Minecraft server. Use your in-game ban command for that too if needed.

Changing roles (Admins)

Find the member in Panel → Members and select Role. Only promote people you trust to Moderator or Admin.

Editing the word filter

  1. Open Panel → Word filter. Words are hidden; select Show words to see them.
  2. To add a word, type it, choose Whole word only or Anywhere, and select Add.
    • Whole word only is best for short words (for example, "cat" is blocked but "category" is fine).
    • Anywhere blocks it even inside other words. Use it for longer words that never appear innocently.
  3. To remove a word, select the × (remove) button next to it.

Changes apply within a minute. The filter automatically catches tricks like c@ts, caaats and c A t (if "cats" were on the list). Links, emails, phone numbers, addresses and other personal information are always blocked and don't need to be added.

Reading the audit log

Panel → Audit log lists every staff action, newest first: who did it, to whom, when, and the reason. Deleted messages show what they said. Nothing can be removed from the audit log, so it keeps staff accountable. Select Load older entries to go further back.

Voice chat

  • Mute in voice: on the Voice page, select Manage next to a player, then Mute in voice and choose a length. They can still listen, but nobody hears them. You can also do this in Panel → Members with Voice mute.
  • Remove from voice: Manage → Remove from voice disconnects them right away. They can rejoin, so mute or ban them if it keeps happening.
  • Groups: staff can see and join every group, including ones with a password.
  • Global Mic: Moderators and Admins can use it. Admins can give it to event hosts with Give Global Mic (Panel → Members, or Manage on the Voice page).
  • Every voice mute, removal and Global Mic change appears in the audit log. Voice is never recorded.

Ranks (Admins and server operators)

  • On the website: Panel → Ranks. Select New rank to choose a name, a prefix (shown as [PREFIX]), a color, an icon (crown, star and more), the commands it can use and its staff powers. Pick a gradient (like Galaxy, which matches the logo, Gold Rush or Rainbow) to give each letter of the prefix and name its own color. To give someone a rank, find them under Players and pick it from the dropdown.
  • In game: server operators type /ranks for menus to give, remove, edit and create ranks.
  • Changes reach the server within seconds: the prefix appears in chat and above the player's head, and their commands update straight away.
  • Ranks are the roles everywhere. The same rank (tag, colors, icon) shows in game and on the website. A rank's Staff powers (None, Moderator or Admin) decide what its players can do on the website. You can also give ranks from Panel → Members. The owners are always Admins.
  • Commands: type a command's name (like time, weather or tp) and press Enter. Players with the rank can use the whole command without being operators, so time also allows /time set day.
  • Only give commands to people you trust. Every rank change is in the audit log.

Economy (Admins and server operators)

  • Every new player gets $1,000. Their money shows at the bottom of the screen and under their name tag. Operators have infinite money (∞).
  • Players: /money, /pay <player> <amount> (amounts like 2.5k or 1m work), /baltop, /shop, /sell (menu), /sell hand, /sell all and /ah. Named or enchanted items are never sold by /sell.
  • Auction House: hold an item and type /ah sell <price>. Listings last 48 hours and a 5% fee is taken when an item sells. Unsold items wait in My listings.
  • Operators in game: /setmoney, /addmoney and /takemoney <player> <amount>.
  • Website: Panel → Economy changes anyone's money (even offline, while the server is online) and edits shop categories, items and prices. Leave a price empty to stop buying or selling that item.
  • Shop NPCs: stand where it should go and type /shopnpc. Tap it to edit its name, what it says, which menus it opens (Shop, Sell, Auction House, Leaderboard, Balance) and its look (villagers, golems, Minecraft's NPC outfits and more), move it or remove it. Sneak and tap to use it like a player. NPCs don't move or take damage.

Help menu and teleports

  • Players type /guide in game for the Help menu: Server Information (with the GalaxySMP logo), Rules and Commands. Tapping a command shows an example. Everyone is reminded about /guide when they join.
  • Edit it: Panel → Help menu (Admins). Change the info text, rules and command list; put **two stars** around words for bold. The website's Rules page shows the same rules.
  • Teleports: /tpa, /tpahere, /tpaccept, /tpdeny, /spawn, /sethome, /home, /homes, /delhome and /back. Players wait 3 seconds without moving; operators teleport instantly. Everyone can have up to 3 homes.
  • Set spawn first: an operator stands at spawn and types /setspawn, or /spawn won't work.

Plans (Galaxy+ and Galaxy Pro+)

  • The plans are listed on the Store page. Paid checkout isn't open yet.
  • Give a plan: Panel → Economy → find the player → Plan. Choose Galaxy+ or Galaxy Pro+ and how long it lasts (or no end date). Choose Galaxy Free to remove it.
  • Paid plans also cost $1,000 of in-game money every 30 days, taken automatically in game. If the player can't pay, the plan's perks pause (they're told once an hour) until they have the money. Operators never pay.
  • Perks in game: Free has 1 home and 10 Auction House listings, Galaxy+ has 5 homes, 20 listings, an aurora [GALAXY+] tag and the Sparkle trail; Pro+ has 10 homes, 30 listings, a royal [PRO+] tag, the Sparkle and Galaxy trails and a join firework. Gold is kept for the Admin rank. Support and event perks are handled by staff.
  • Players type /plan to see their plan and when it ends.

Plots and protected areas

  • Get the Plot Axe: type /plotaxe (operators only).
  • Make a plot or area: left-click the ground for the first corner and right-click the opposite corner. The Save menu opens: choose Plot (players can claim it) or Protected area (like spawn), the height above the ground (it always goes all the way down) and who can claim it. Protected areas need a name and let you choose whether players can use doors, open chests, fight, and whether explosions break blocks.
  • Saving takes about 5 seconds. Plots get a white concrete edge while they're available. Plots can be up to 128 x 128 blocks and can't overlap.
  • Edit or delete: sneak and right-click inside a plot or area with the Plot Axe. You can change settings, unclaim and reset a player's plot, or delete it. /plot list lists them all.
  • Players: everyone gets a Plot Claiming Shovel when they first join. Right-clicking the ground inside an available plot claims it, the shovel breaks, and they get a Plot Manager (clock) to add Builders and Visitors by gamertag, highlight the border, teleport there or unclaim. Unclaiming resets the plot to exactly how it was when it was made.
  • How many plots: Galaxy Free 1, Galaxy+ 3, Pro+ 5, plus any extra plots. Give extra plots in Panel → Economy → Plan. A lost shovel can be replaced with /plot shovel if the player still has a free plot.
  • Outside plots and protected areas, everyone can build as normal. Remove the old PlotGuard plugin so the two don't clash.

Community channels

  • #Information shows the Server Information and rules from Panel → Help menu. Edit them there.
  • #Announcements is for staff posts. #Events says "coming soon" until events are connected to the game.
  • #Auction House shows the in-game Auction House live. Players buy with their in-game money and must be online in Minecraft; the item goes straight into their inventory.
  • #Suggestions: players post ideas (optionally anonymously) and like or dislike them. Staff can mark ideas Open, Planned, Done or Declined, and delete them. Only the owners can see who wrote an anonymous suggestion.
  • Voice Chat is the voice page inside the community. It keeps running while you read other channels.
  • The old #help, #off-topic and game-mode channels are hidden. Their messages are kept, and #general is now #Chat.

Minigame Center

  • Computers: place Minigame Computer blocks (iMac style) at the desk: find it in the creative inventory (Items) or type /give @s galaxysmp:computer. Right-clicking one opens the Minigame Center. /minigames opens it too.
  • Set up your minigames (operators): Minigame Center → Minigame Staff → Set up minigames → Add a minigame, then stand in its backstage room (where players wait) and its entrance (where watchers go) and set each one.
  • Minigame Staff: tick Minigame Staff on a rank (Panel → Ranks, or /ranks). They get a message when someone asks to host, and get an extra Minigame Staff button on the computers (everyone else sees the same menu without it). It has Host requests to accept (with the host's notes) or decline with a reason. Operators are always Minigame Staff.
  • Hosting is for Galaxy+ and Pro+ players. They pick a minigame (or Custom with their own name), who can join (Everyone, Galaxy+ and Pro+, or Pro+ only) and how long sign-ups stay open. When you accept, sign-ups open, players who can join are told, and the host types /tpd to teleport to you.
  • Custom minigames: where you stand when you accept becomes the backstage room and entrance. Change them in Minigame Staff → Manage minigames, which also closes sign-ups early or ends a game. Hosts can end their own game by pressing Host a Minigame again. Games end by themselves after 3 hours.
  • Players Join (teleported backstage) while sign-ups are open, or Watch (teleported to the entrance). Running games also show in #Events on the website.

Daily rewards, quests, achievements and leaderboards

  • /daily: $100 on day 1, $200 on day 2 … $700 plus a crate key on day 7, then it starts over. Missing a day restarts at day 1. Galaxy+ gets 25% more, Galaxy Pro+ 50% more. Days start at midnight US Eastern.
  • /quests: every player gets 3 quests a day: easy ($25), medium ($75) and hard ($200), like "Mine 64 stone" or "Mine 5 diamond ore".
  • /achievements: 24 badges (playtime, mining, building, mobs, streaks, quests, money…), some with small money rewards. Badges show on the player's profile.
  • Rewards are kept small on purpose so the economy stays realistic.
  • Profiles: galaxymc.us/player/Name shows anyone's rank, plan, money, playtime, stats and badges. Search for players on the Leaderboards page.
  • Leaderboards: galaxymc.us/leaderboard ranks money, playtime, mining, building, mobs, quests, streaks and badges.
  • Floating leaderboards: stand where you want one (e.g. at spawn), type /leaderboard, choose Place one here and what it ranks. They update every minute. Remove the nearest deletes one within 5 blocks. Needs the GalaxySMP blocks pack 1.0.6+.

Jobs, the Shop District and crates

  • Jobs (/jobs): Miner, Lumberjack, Farmer, Hunter, Digger or Builder. Small pay per action (e.g. stone $0.05, diamond ore $3), up to 2x at level 21. First job free, switching costs $250. Players can earn up to $300 a day from jobs. Blocks players placed don't pay when broken.
  • Shop District: make shop plots with the Plot Axe: choose Shop plot (Shop District) when saving. Everyone gets shop plots separately from home plots: Galaxy Free 1, Galaxy+ 2, Galaxy Pro+ 3. Players claim them with the Plot Claiming Shovel.
  • Shop chests: in their shop plot, players hold an item and sneak + tap a chest or barrel to make a shop chest that sells (or buys) that item. Stock is stored inside the shop, so it can't be stolen. Money goes to the owner even when they're offline. Owners name their shop and place a floating sign from the Plot Manager. Unclaiming or deleting a shop plot gives all stock back to the owner.
  • Crates: Common, Rare and Galaxy. /crates → Place a crate, pick it, then tap the block. Edit prizes and chances there too (add the item in your hand, or money). Keys: Common from finishing all 3 daily quests or buying one in the Shop ($500, change it in /crates); Rare from day 7 of /daily; Galaxy from staff and events (/givekey Name galaxy 1, works from the console).

Smooth gradient rank tags

  • Bedrock text only has 28 colors, so in game gradient rank tags (and the Galaxy+ / Pro+ tags) are drawn as small pictures with a truly smooth gradient, like the rank icons.
  • The plugin makes them by itself and saves them as BDS-Addons/GalaxySMP_RankTags.mcpack. New or changed gradient ranks get their pictures after the next server restart; until then they use the normal colors.
  • /ranktags off switches back to normal colored text right away, /ranktags on turns the pictures back on. Player names keep the 28-color gradient.

Elevators

  • A real moving cabin: the cabin floor moves up and down the shaft (about 2 blocks a second) and carries everyone standing on it. Iron doors next to the shaft close before it leaves and open with a ding when it arrives.
  • Elevator Tool: staff, Galaxy+ and Galaxy Pro+ players automatically have an Elevator Tool (a nether star) in their inventory. It's taken away if their plan ends; /elevator gives it back if they lose it.
  • Build: make a shaft (up to 5x5 inside) with the cabin floor at the bottom. Use the Elevator Tool → Create an elevator → tap two opposite corners of the cabin floor. On each other floor, stand on the landing next to the shaft → Elevator Tool → Add a floor here.
  • Use: a button next to the shaft calls it to that floor. Inside, tap the cabin floor to choose a floor.
  • Staff can build elevators anywhere. Galaxy+ players can build 2 and Galaxy Pro+ 4, inside their own plots. Keep the shaft empty or the elevator says it's blocked.

In-game reports and moderation

  • Players type /report (or /report Name) to report someone. Staff in game get an alert straight away, and the report appears in Panel → Reports under "In-game reports", with where it happened and the player's past warnings, mutes and bans.
  • /mod opens the moderation panel: online players, find any player (even offline), open reports, muted players and block logs.
  • For each player you can Warn, Mute (10 minutes to 30 days; they can't chat, /msg or /me), Kick, Ban (1 day, 7 days, 30 days or permanent), see their History, teleport to them, or see their block changes.
  • From a report you can moderate the player, teleport to where it was reported, and mark it resolved or dismiss it. Reports can also be resolved on the website.
  • Who can do what: operators can do everything. Give a rank the mod command for warn/mute/kick/reports/inspector, modban to also ban, and rollback to roll back griefing. Staff can't moderate operators or other staff (operators can).
  • Every action shows in the Audit log as "Name (in game)". Mutes and bans still work if the website is down; the log catches up when it's back.

Anti-grief: inspector and rollback

  • The server remembers who placed or broke every block for 14 days.
  • /inspect turns the inspector on. Break or tap a block to see who changed it and when (the block isn't changed). Place a block to check an empty spot where something was broken. /inspect again to turn it off.
  • /rollback (or /rollback Name): pick the player, how far back (10 minutes to 14 days) and where (20, 50 or 100 blocks around you, or everywhere). You'll see how many blocks will change before you confirm. Broken blocks come back and placed blocks disappear.
  • Spots that another player changed afterwards are left alone. Chest contents and TNT/creeper damage can't be restored. Stand near the damage so the area is loaded.

In-game announcements

  • Type /announcement (or /ann) in game to open the announcement panel (operators, or a rank given the announce command).
  • Announce now: type a message. Everyone hears a chime and sees it in a dark galaxy box at the top of the screen for 30 seconds. Colour codes like §3 work. Shortcut: /ann Your message here.
  • Auto announcements: add, edit or remove tips that are announced at random (default every 10 minutes, change it in Settings, or turn them off).
  • Two announcements never show at once. A new one waits in line until the current one is gone; typed announcements go before auto ones.
  • The box needs the GalaxySMP icons pack. Without it the message shows as a normal title in the middle of the screen.

In-game chat filter

  • When it's on, bad words in Minecraft chat are scrambled (Minecraft's §k effect). The rest of the message still sends. The real word never reaches anyone's chat.
  • It uses the same word list as the website (Panel → Word filter) and catches the same tricks: c@ts, caaats, c A t (if "cats" were on the list).
  • Turn it on or off for everyone: the switch at the top of Panel → Word filter (Moderators and Admins), or /chatfilter on / /chatfilter off in game (operators, or a rank given the chatfilter command). It starts on. Every change is in the audit log.

Good habits

  • Always add a reason. It helps other staff understand what happened.
  • Stay calm and kind, even when others aren't.
  • If a player shares something worrying about their safety, tell the server owner right away.
  • Never ask players for personal information, and never share a player's details.
