Join voice as

Your browser will ask to use your microphone. You join muted; unmute when you're ready to talk.

Playing on the same phone? Join here first, then switch to Minecraft: voice keeps running in the background (you'll see it in your notifications / lock screen). For the best results install the GalaxySMP app and use voice from it. On iPhone, if others can't hear you after switching, use voice on a second device.

How it works

  • Proximity: players within 48 blocks hear you, quieter with distance and from the direction you're standing.
  • Groups: talk with friends from anywhere. Groups can be open or have a password.
  • Global Mic: staff and event hosts can talk to everyone at once.
  • Private: voice is never recorded, and other players never see your internet address.
