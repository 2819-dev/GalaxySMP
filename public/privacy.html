Privacy Policy

We collect as little information as possible. This page explains exactly what we store and why.

Information we store

  • Minecraft username and player ID, to identify your account.
  • Your password, encrypted with bcrypt. It cannot be read by anyone, including staff.
  • Community activity: messages, reactions and reports you submit.
  • Notifications: if you allow notifications, we store your device's notification address (from your browser or phone) and your Notification Preferences, only to send you the GalaxySMP notifications you chose. Turn them off any time in Notification Preferences.
  • Direct messages: private messages you send to other members (on the website or with /dm in game) are stored so both of you can read them. To keep players safe, the server owners can read any direct message, for example to look into a report.
  • Moderation records, such as mutes and bans, to keep the community safe.
  • Activity on your profile: like Discord, your profile card shows when you're playing on GalaxySMP. If you play on Bedrock with an Xbox account, it can also show what you're doing on Xbox Live (for example "Playing Minecraft on Xbox Series X|S"), which we look up from your gamertag through OpenXBL and keep for a few minutes. Turn the Xbox part off any time in Account → Privacy.
  • Welcome steps: when you first sign in we ask for your first and last name, what you like to play, an optional short bio and a profile color (shown on your profile), and how you found GalaxySMP and who invited you (only staff see these). You choose whether everyone or only you and staff can see your name, and you can change it any time in Account → Privacy.
  • Real name (only if staff add it): staff can add your real name to your account. It shows on your profile card next to your username. You can hide it from other players any time in Account → Privacy; staff can still see it. Ask staff to remove it completely.

Voice chat

  • Voice is never recorded or stored. It travels through Cloudflare's voice relay straight to the players allowed to hear you, so other players never see your internet address.
  • While you're in voice, the server shares your in-game position with the voice system so nearby players sound louder. Other players only see how far away you are, not your coordinates.

In-game money

  • The server keeps each player's in-game money balance (it isn't real money). The public Money Leaderboard shows the top players' gamertags and balances.

Store payments and Galaxy Credits

  • Card payments are handled by Stripe on Stripe's own secure checkout page. We never see or store your card number. Stripe asks for what it needs to take the payment (such as card details, an email address for the receipt, and a billing country or postcode) and keeps it under Stripe's Privacy Policy.
  • From Stripe we only keep what we need to give you what you bought: which website account paid, what for, how much, and whether the payment went through or was refunded.
  • We keep your Galaxy Credits balance and a history of top-ups, purchases and gifts (including who sent or received a gift), so your balance always adds up.
  • Donations are made on our server host BisectHosting's own page, under their privacy policy. We don't receive any details about who donated.
  • Players under 18 should ask a parent or guardian before buying or donating anything.

Information we never collect

  • Email addresses, birthdays, phone numbers, home addresses or photos (apart from what you give Stripe when you pay by card, which Stripe keeps, not us). Real names are only stored if staff add one, as described above.
  • Advertising, tracking or analytics data. The website loads no third-party content; when you pay by card you're sent to Stripe's page.

Cookies

We use a single cookie to keep you signed in. It is not used for any other purpose.

Deleting your account

To have your account and messages deleted, contact a staff member in game or in #Support, and an administrator will remove them.
