Privacy

We keep as little about you as possible. This page lists everything we store.

What we store

  • Your Minecraft username and player ID, so we know which player you are.
  • Your password, scrambled (bcrypt). Nobody can read it, not even staff.
  • Messages you post in the community chat, plus reactions and reports.
  • Moderation history, such as mutes and bans, so staff can keep chat safe.

What we never ask for

  • No email, real name, birthday, phone number, address or photos.
  • No ads, no trackers and no analytics. The website doesn't load anything from other companies.

Cookies

When you log in, we save one cookie so you stay logged in. That's the only cookie, and it's only used for logging in.

Want your account deleted?

Ask a staff member in the game or in #help, and an Admin will delete your account and messages.
