Privacy Policy

We collect as little information as possible. This page explains exactly what we store and why.

Information we store

  • Minecraft username and player ID, to identify your account.
  • Your password, encrypted with bcrypt. It cannot be read by anyone, including staff.
  • Community activity: messages, reactions and reports you submit.
  • Moderation records, such as mutes and bans, to keep the community safe.

Voice chat

  • Voice is never recorded or stored. It travels through Cloudflare's voice relay straight to the players allowed to hear you, so other players never see your internet address.
  • While you're in voice, the server shares your in-game position with the voice system so nearby players sound louder. Other players only see how far away you are, not your coordinates.

In-game money

  • The server keeps each player's in-game money balance (it isn't real money). The public Money Leaderboard shows the top players' gamertags and balances.

Information we never collect

  • Email addresses, real names, birthdays, phone numbers, home addresses or photos.
  • Advertising, tracking or analytics data. The website loads no third-party content.

Cookies

We use a single cookie to keep you signed in. It is not used for any other purpose.

Deleting your account

To have your account and messages deleted, contact a staff member in game or in #help, and an administrator will remove them.
