Questions. Everything you need to know.

How do ranks work?

Each purchase gives 30 days, and buying again adds 30 more. Paid ranks also cost $1K of in-game money each month, taken automatically when you're online. If you don't have it, your perks pause until you do. Type /plan in game to see yours.

When do I get what I bought?

Right away if you're online, or the next time you join. Money goes straight to your balance, and you can pick cosmetics with /cosmetics. Shop items are yours to keep (Night Vision lasts the time you choose).

What are Galaxy Credits?

Store money you can spend on anything here or gift to other members. They can't be swapped back for real money.

I have a promo code

Type it at checkout. If staff made one just for you, checkout tells you about it.

Is paying safe?

Card payments are handled by Stripe on its own secure page, and we never see your card number. Donations are handled by our server host, BisectHosting.

What does "Coming soon" mean?

Those things can't be bought yet. Staff may give them out for events and giveaways.

Under 18? Ask a parent or guardian before buying or donating anything.
