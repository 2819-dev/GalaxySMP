How it works

  • Paid plans last 30 days per purchase and also cost $1K of in-game money each month. The in-game part is taken automatically when you're online; if you don't have it, your perks pause until you do.
  • Plans are linked to your Minecraft account. Type /plan in game to see yours.
  • Shop items are yours to keep (Night Vision lasts the time you choose). Pick cosmetics in game with /cosmetics. Money is added to your balance straight away.
  • Things marked Coming soon can't be bought yet. Staff may give them for events and giveaways.
  • Got a promo code? Type it at checkout. If staff made one just for you, checkout tells you.
  • Galaxy Credits can be spent on anything in the Store or gifted to other members. They can't be swapped back for real money.
  • Card payments are handled by Stripe on its own secure page. We never see your card number.
  • Donations are handled by our server host, BisectHosting, on their own page.
  • Players under 18 should ask a parent or guardian before buying or donating anything.
