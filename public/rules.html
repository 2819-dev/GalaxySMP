Rules

These rules apply in game and in the website chat. Staff may warn, mute or ban players who break them.

Summary

  1. Be kind. No bullying, mean names or hate.
  2. Keep it clean. No swearing or grown-up stuff.
  3. Stay safe. Never share your real name, address, school, phone number or socials.
  4. No griefing, stealing or ruining other people's builds.
  5. No cheats, hacked clients or exploiting bugs.
  6. Listen to staff. If something feels wrong, report it.

Full rules

1. Be kind to everyone

  • No bullying or harassment. Don't pick on, follow around or gang up on other players.
  • No hate. Nothing mean about anyone's race, religion, gender, disability or who they are.
  • No drama. If you have a problem with someone, tell a staff member instead of arguing.

2. Keep chat clean

  • No swearing, including spelling words weirdly to get around the filter.
  • No grown-up topics, scary content or anything not okay for young players.
  • No spam. Don't flood chat, use all caps or repeat the same message.
  • No advertising other servers, channels or websites.

3. Stay safe online

  • Never share personal information: your real name, age, address, school, phone number, photos or social media.
  • Never ask other people for their personal information.
  • Don't move chats to other apps. If someone asks you to talk somewhere else, tell a staff member.
  • Never share your password, not even with friends or "staff". Real staff will never ask for it.

4. Play fair

  • No griefing: don't break, change or steal from builds that aren't yours.
  • No cheating: no hacked clients, X-ray, auto-clickers or mods that give an unfair advantage.
  • No exploiting bugs. Found one? Report it to staff and you might get a thank-you!
  • No scamming or tricking players out of their items.

5. Respect staff

  • Follow staff instructions. If you disagree, ask politely in #help.
  • Don't pretend to be staff.
  • Use the Report button for problems instead of fighting back.

What happens if you break a rule

Staff choose what's fair for what happened: a warning, a mute (you can't chat for a while), or a ban for serious or repeated problems.
