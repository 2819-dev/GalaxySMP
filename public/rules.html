Rules

These rules apply on the server and in community chat. Staff may issue warnings, mutes or bans at their discretion.

Summary

  1. Treat everyone with respect. Bullying, harassment and hate are not tolerated.
  2. Keep chat appropriate for all ages. No swearing or mature content.
  3. Never share personal information, whether it's yours or someone else's.
  4. No griefing, stealing or damaging other players' builds.
  5. No cheats, modified clients or exploits.
  6. Follow staff instructions, and report problems instead of retaliating.

Full rules

1. Respect

  • No bullying or harassment. This includes name-calling, targeting a player repeatedly, or encouraging others to do so.
  • No hate speech. Comments about anyone's race, religion, gender, disability or identity are not allowed.
  • Resolve disputes through staff. If you have a problem with another player, contact a staff member rather than arguing in public.

2. Chat

  • No swearing, including misspellings or symbols used to get around the filter.
  • No mature or frightening content. Keep all topics suitable for younger players.
  • No spam. Avoid flooding chat, excessive capital letters or repeated messages.
  • No advertising of other servers, channels or websites.

3. Online safety

  • Keep personal information private: real names, ages, addresses, schools, phone numbers, photos and social media accounts.
  • Never ask other players for their personal information.
  • Keep conversations on GalaxySMP. If someone asks you to move to another app, tell a staff member.
  • Never share your password. Staff will never ask for it.

4. Fair play

  • No griefing. Do not break, alter or take from builds that aren't yours.
  • No cheating. Hacked clients, X-ray, auto-clickers and other unfair modifications are prohibited.
  • Report bugs instead of exploiting them. Players who report exploits may be rewarded.
  • No scamming or misleading players in trades.

5. Staff

  • Follow staff instructions. If you disagree with a decision, raise it politely in #Support.
  • Do not impersonate staff.
  • Use the Report button to flag problems instead of responding yourself.

Enforcement

Staff decide the appropriate response for each situation. This may be a warning, a temporary mute, or a ban for serious or repeated violations.
