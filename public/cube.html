Play on any console

The Galaxy Cube lets you join GalaxySMP from Xbox, PlayStation and Nintendo Switch, and talk in voice chat with a headset or the built-in microphone.

Setting up your Cube

  1. Plug in your Cube. The screen says Galaxy Cube.
  2. On your phone, join the Wi-Fi called CUBE-XXXX (the name on the screen). A page opens: pick your home Wi-Fi and type its password.
  3. The Cube shows a code like K7Q-29M. Type it above (sign in first).
  4. Turn the knob to your console and press it. The screen shows a DNS address: enter it once in your console's network settings (as the primary DNS). Then open Minecraft, go to Play > Servers, open any featured server, and choose GalaxySMP from the list.
  5. For voice chat, plug a headset into the Cube (or use its mic and speaker). Hold Talk to speak, or press Mute.

Your console and the Cube must be on the same home network. Voice chat uses the Minecraft account linked to your galaxymc.us account.
