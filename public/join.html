On Xbox, PlayStation or Switch? Those consoles don't show an "Add Server" button for every server. Ask a grown-up to help, or ask in the #help channel and our staff will explain the easiest way.

Once you're in

🧭 Pick a world

You start in the hub. Walk up to an NPC (a character standing still) and click it to teleport to Survival, Minigames, Skyblock or Creative.

🔗 Join the website chat

Go to Community and pick a password. You'll get a code. In the game, type /link followed by your code. That's it!

❓
Can't connect? Check that the server is online on the Status page, make sure you typed the address exactly, and update Minecraft to the newest version.
