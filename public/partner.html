What a Network Server gets

A Network Server is a server you fully own. Here's exactly what joining the network means.

You own it

You run your server and your community. Your own owner and admins keep their operator access, and your rules are yours.

GalaxySMP's owner and admins are operators

Our plugin gives GalaxySMP's owner and admins operator on your server, so we can help with moderation and support. That's only our owner and admins, not our moderators or players.

A network logo in the top-right corner

A small logo shows in the top-right corner of every player's screen. It's the network logo, or a custom logo made for your server: a retro games server gets a retro games logo. Tell us your theme when you apply.

Network ranks

GalaxySMP ranks show on your server, in chat and above players' heads, so people are recognized across the network.

No chat sync

Your chat stays on your server. Nothing your players type is sent to GalaxySMP or shown in our chat.

No Main Menu

Players join straight into your world. GalaxySMP's Main Menu isn't added to your server.

What your server needs

  • A Minecraft Bedrock Dedicated Server with Endstone, the free plugin loader our plugin runs on.
  • Two files from us: the GalaxySMP Network plugin and your logo pack, plus a small setup file that connects your server to the network. We send them to you once you're accepted.
  • Your server needs to be able to reach galaxymc.us on the internet.

What the plugin sends us: every minute, the gamertags of the players online (to look up their ranks) and that your server is online. It doesn't send chat, items, locations or anything else.

How it works

  1. Apply below. Tell us about your server. It takes a minute and you don't need an account.
  2. Chat with our staff. A private chat opens right here. Save the link to come back from any device.
  3. Get your kit. If you're accepted, we send your plugin, logo pack and setup file, with a step-by-step guide.
  4. Install and restart. Your server joins the network.
